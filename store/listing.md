**To'liq tavsif:**
Vault Calculator — oddiy va qulay kalkulyator.

• Arifmetik amallar: + − × ÷, daraja, kasr natija
• Muhandislik funksiyalari: sin, cos, tan, log, √, π, x²
• Tenglama yechish: x ni topish (masalan, 2*x+6=20 → x = 7)
• Yorug' va qorong'i rejim, gorizontal ko'rinish
• Internet shart emas — hammasi qurilmada ishlaydi
